## Prompt 4A · Interview prep

````text
Help me prepare for a job interview for the job below. Use ONLY facts from my resume and story bank.

Give me:
1. 8 likely interview questions: some about my past behavior, some about this role.
2. For each question, a sample answer in STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Each answer takes 60 to 90 seconds to say. Simple English. Use my real numbers.
3. 3 smart questions I can ask them.
4. 1 hard question I might get, and a calm, honest way to answer it.

Do not invent experience. If I do not have something the job asks for, say so, and show me how to talk about it honestly.

Privacy: I removed my street address, phone number, and any ID, visa, A-number or case numbers. If you still see any of these below, do not use them, and tell me to remove them.

[PASTE THE JOB POSTING HERE]

````
